# Audience and problem

Status: Draft

## Initial audience hypothesis

**Proposed starting audience:** multilingual couples and visiting relatives who regularly spend time together but do not share a fluent language.

This is a candidate, not a settled customer segment. It offers recurring face-to-face situations and a concrete opportunity to observe whether better conversation matters. The buyer might be the person connecting two language communities; the participants may never purchase or create accounts themselves.

## Core job

When I am with someone whose language I do not speak well, help us exchange meaning and repair misunderstandings so we can participate in a real conversation without relying on another person to interpret every turn.

## Problems to investigate

- Setup and switching languages interrupt the social moment.
- Participants cannot tell whose turn it is or whether the app is listening.
- A fluent mistranslation is accepted because nobody can inspect it easily.
- Passing a phone, poor orientation, background noise, or simultaneous speech makes participation uneven.
- A conversation ends before a mistaken name, number, or intention is corrected.

These are research hypotheses. No frequency or severity has been measured for Trio.

## Alternative starting audiences

| Audience | Candidate situation | Potential payer | What we must establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Couples and relatives | Recurring personal conversations | Household member | Frequency, comfort, repeat use, willingness to pay |
| Travelers | Brief practical exchanges | Traveler | Distribution, episodic demand, offline needs |
| Small hospitality teams | Staff and guest communication | Business | Workflow fit, procurement, reliability expectations |
| International social groups | Shared activities with several languages | Host or participant | Turn-taking, group audio quality, participant onboarding |
| Language learners | Understand and prepare expressions | Learner | Distinct value beyond existing learning habits |

Choose one initial audience after interviews and observed tasks. Do not combine all of these into an undifferentiated launch customer.

## Alternatives to understand

Research what participants actually do: existing translation tools, a bilingual friend, gestures, simplified speech, messaging, avoiding the conversation, or using a shared second language. Record concrete tasks and results rather than unsupported competitor comparisons.

## Evidence needed before committing

Interview people from a narrow segment, observe recent real situations, test the complete conversation journey, and run a clearly described paid pilot or pricing experiment. Capture where the experience succeeds, fails, or adds no value. [Research practice](research/README.md) defines how findings enter the specification.
