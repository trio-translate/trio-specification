# Go to market

Status: Draft

## Initial positioning hypothesis

For people who regularly spend time with loved ones across a language gap, Trio helps both people follow, reply, and repair a conversation on a shared device.

This positioning follows the proposed initial audience. Revise it if [audience research](../audience-and-problem.md) points elsewhere. Do not claim superior accuracy or universal language support without evidence.

## First distribution experiments

1. Recruit through people already experiencing the selected situation, using individual opt-in invitations and relevant communities where recruitment is welcome.
2. Demonstrate a complete exchange, including correction and stopping, with consented or synthetic content.
3. Give participants a clear pilot offer and an easy feedback path.
4. Observe whether they return for another real conversation and invite a relevant person voluntarily.

These are proposed experiments, not authorization to contact people, publish campaigns, or spend money.

## Experiment record

For each channel, record target segment, message, offer, recruitment method, effort/cost, eligible participants, activation, repeat use, paid conversion, and support burden. Keep denominators and the observation window explicit. Compare acquired customers and successful situations, not only traffic or installs.

## Launch materials to write later

Product demonstration; concise supported-language/device list; value and limitations; pricing/allowance explanation; privacy and capture explanation; onboarding; support/help content; and relevant store/website content for the selected distribution channel.

Distribution ownership, launch markets, channel budget, and any partnerships remain open.
