# Commercialization and launch evidence

Status: Draft; audience, price, budgets and willingness to pay unvalidated

## Choose a beachhead

Keep the broad vision while testing one complete paid use case. The current [audience hypothesis](../audience-and-problem.md) is recurring family/couple conversation. Offline passenger/driver use is a required product situation, with launch assignment still open. Research both before assuming the car situation is the strongest paying audience.

| Hypothesis | Evidence to collect | Decision it informs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The language gap causes a recurring costly/frustrating problem | Recent concrete examples, frequency, workarounds and failed attempts | Initial situation and audience |
| Trio offers enough additional value over available alternatives | Observe the same task with their current alternative and Trio; count touches, repair and successful understanding | Positioning and differentiated promise |
| Someone will pay for this outcome | Specific transparent offer, actual purchase/paid pilot and later continuation | Payer, packaging and price |
| Serving that customer is sustainable | Processing/license/support cost distribution including twelve-hour heavy use | Margin, allowances and architecture |
| We can reach customers efficiently | Small channel experiment with eligibility, activation, retention and acquisition cost | Distribution and affordable growth |

Do not treat AI actors, compliments, survey intentions, competitor language counts, or a successful demo as payment evidence. Record refusals and failed situations as well as successes. Set decision thresholds and observation windows before assessing an experiment; all numeric targets remain TBD.

## From first interview to paid continuation

1. Prepare a research brief and consented task based on one situation. Record current alternatives and buying responsibility without collecting unnecessary conversation content.
2. Use an honest complete demo with exact device/language/offline limitations. Test whether participants can start, understand, repair and stop it themselves.
3. Present one simple pilot package from the [business model](business-model.md), including required downloads, supported configurations, allowance and total price. Price/currency are still decisions, not assumed demand.
4. Before charging, verify purchase, receipt, entitlement, refund/cancellation and support behavior in the chosen channel. Offline runtime and twelve-hour continuity must survive the commercial design.
5. Run a small opt-in pilot; measure successful situations per household, repeat use, paid continuation, support contacts and cost. Analyze cohorts and denominators, not installs alone.
6. Expand only when the complete experience and economics warrant it. Preserve support capacity and documented rollback. Update actual positioning and the support matrix together.

## Economics and experiments

Use light, typical and heavy scenarios; distinguish purchased device/license costs, downloads/distribution, active speech, continuous idle monitoring, translation fan-out, TTS, retries, infrastructure and support. Compare local, hybrid and cloud candidates only under equivalent quality and outcome constraints. Include acquisition cost and refunds; a healthy processing margin alone is not profitability.

Use the [commercial experiment template](../../templates/commercial-experiment.md) to record hypothesis, population, offer, channel, time window, spend cap, success/failure threshold, evidence and next decision. No pilot outreach, ad spend, subscriptions or publication occurs just because this plan exists.

## Launch evidence packet

Include exact supported devices/languages/routes/situations, passing product gates, known exclusions, understandable pricing, content/capture policy, purchase and cancellation verification, support owner, incident/rollback procedure, measured cost envelope, and a factual demo. Any legal/store/tax/privacy obligations depend on launch jurisdiction and channel; investigate current authoritative requirements when those are selected instead of inventing universal rules here.

Every material marketing claim needs its source and scope: vendor-documented, internally measured, participant-observed or hypothesis. Keep a claim ledger alongside [competitor analysis](competitor-analysis.md). A regression in a required capability blocks renewing the affected claim, regardless of a favorable weighted score.
