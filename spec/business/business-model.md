# Business model

Status: Draft

## Value and payer hypothesis

For the proposed family/couple audience, a household member may pay to make recurring conversations easier. The other participant should be able to benefit without becoming a separate paying customer unless research supports that requirement.

No price, conversion rate, willingness to pay, or market size has been validated.

## Packaging hypotheses

| Model | Why it might fit | What could make it fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription with clear allowance | Recurring family/social use | Infrequent use, confusing metering, expensive heavy users |
| Prepaid allowance or time-limited pass | Visits and episodic use | Payment friction and weak repeat use |
| Business plan | A repeated service workflow | Different customer, sales cycle, support, and trust burden |

Choose one pilot offer for the selected audience. Do not implement several billing models before testing the need. Avoid promising unlimited AI processing without measured cost distributions and enforceable economics.

Packaging must satisfy the confirmed one-action, twelve-hour continuous experience. Settle sufficient pre-session entitlement and the economics of extended operation; an unexpected quota cutoff or forced renewal prompt is not an acceptable solution. This product constraint does not itself establish unlimited pricing or authorize unbounded provider spend. Offline car sessions must not depend on a live entitlement server during use.

## Unit economics

For each paid period, estimate and then measure:

`Contribution = collected revenue - taxes/refunds/payment fees - variable language processing - variable infrastructure - variable support`

Break processing into recognition, translation, speech generation, retries, and participant/language fan-out where applicable. Track idle session time separately from active speech; a billed minute must have an explicit definition. Separate fixed development costs from recurring service economics.

Model the twelve-hour case, including continuous word observation, time with no speech, music/noise processing, connection renewal, and heavy speech use. For local processing, measure device resource use plus distribution/storage/support or licensing costs where applicable. Music-muted TTS does not necessarily eliminate recognition/translation work.

Use scenarios with clearly labeled assumptions for light, typical, and heavy users. Choose the currency and numerical inputs after measuring candidate processing and researching the audience. Git activity and token guesses are not billing evidence.

## Commercial experiment

Recruit a narrow cohort around a real recurring situation. Present a specific offer and allowance, then test actual use and payment with clear terms. Record completion, repeat usage, paid continuation, support burden, and cost per successful situation. Define decision thresholds before evaluating the results.

## Decisions before charging

Payer identity; purchase channel; price/currency; allowance unit; counting precision; pre-limit warning; treatment of an in-progress turn; renewal/cancellation/refund behavior; failed-payment behavior; entitlement enforcement; and customer support ownership.
