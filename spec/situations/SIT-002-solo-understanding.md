# SIT-002: Understanding or preparing language alone

Status: Draft
Scope: Future candidate

## People, trigger, and outcome

A person encounters language they do not understand or wants to prepare a message in another language. They need a faithful translation and, when requested, an explanation they can distinguish from the original meaning.

## Proposed journey

1. Choose source and target languages, then type or deliberately capture speech.
2. Review the source and translation.
3. Request an optional explanation or alternative phrasing; generated additions are visibly separate.
4. Correct the source or specify intended context.
5. Copy the chosen result or end the session. Saving is a separate explicit action if supported.

## Failure and recovery

Unsupported language, ambiguous phrases, uncertain names, and missing context should lead to a clear limitation or clarification path. A suggested response must not be represented as something the other person said. Copying text must not automatically send it to another person.

## Completion and acceptance direction

The person can explain the source's meaning or select wording that matches their intention. Test whether explanations preserve the distinction between literal content, interpretation, and suggested wording.
