# DEC-001: Use Mellom as a starting point and broaden the vision

Status: Agreed
Date: 2026-09-12
Decision owner: Product owner

## Context

The product owner requested a full specification workspace for an ambitious language, translator, and mutual-understanding app under the working name Trio. Earlier Mellom planning provides relevant product context.

## Decision

Use Mellom as a starting point and broaden the vision.

## Evidence

In the setup conversation on 2026-09-12, the product owner selected: "Use it as a starting point, and broaden the vision."

## Alternatives considered

Treat Trio as a completely fresh product, or make Trio the new name for Mellom. The selected direction allows continuity while leaving room to rethink and extend the product.

## Consequences

- Use earlier conversation and understanding concepts as inputs to Trio's vision and situation catalog.
- Explore solo, paired, group, and broader contexts while choosing a focused first release.
- Do not assume that prior code, vendors, language choices, platforms, branding, or completion claims have been accepted for Trio.
- Review existing implementation separately before deciding reuse.

Affected documents: [vision](../vision.md), [scope](../scope-and-roadmap.md), [architecture](../technical/architecture.md), and repository [working instructions](../../AGENTS.md).

Supersedes: None.
