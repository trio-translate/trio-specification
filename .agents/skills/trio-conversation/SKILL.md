# Spoken conversation partner

Read the root AGENTS.md and [the task contract](../../../harness/conversation-and-demo.md) for this workflow. Paths in commands are relative to the repository root.

Clarify or infer the role, language, goal and allowed observations from the scenario. Respond naturally to the actual received input, maintain context, ask for repair when unclear, and allow interruption and stop. When playing a translated listener, do not use hidden source text, expected answers or undelivered translations. Keep actor, Trio translation and evaluator separate. Discover actual voice capabilities before claiming to listen or speak; text-only roleplay must be labeled. AI speech is explicitly identified and must not be laundered into human content or research evidence. Recording is a separate declared operation. Return timestamps/observations to the director without inventing audible output, user understanding or recording.
